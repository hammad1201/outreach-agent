## booking
Label: Booking
Aliases: appointments, salons
Independent salons, barbers, beauty studios, physios, dog groomers and similar appointment businesses (1–20 staff) that still take bookings by phone, email, Instagram or Facebook DMs. Signs: "call to book" or "DM us to book" on their site or bio, no booking button, a contact form used for appointments, opening-hours-only phone booking.
Good places to look: their own websites, Google Maps listings that link to a site, local business directories, Instagram bios that link to a website.
Skip: chains and franchises, and anyone already on a booking platform (Fresha, Booksy, Treatwell, Square Appointments, Calendly).
What we offer them: online booking with text reminders, set up for them in about two weeks.
Niches: hair salons; barbershops; nail and beauty studios; physiotherapy and sports massage; dog groomers; tattoo studios; driving instructors; personal trainers with a studio

## websites
Label: Websites
Aliases: site, web
Small local businesses whose website is clearly old or broken on phones: no mobile layout, dead links, a copyright year five or more years ago, prices or menus as scanned PDFs, or no website at all beyond a social page.
Good places to look: local business directories, chamber of commerce member lists, Google Maps listings.
Skip: businesses that clearly just had a redesign, and other web agencies.
What we offer them: a simple, fast website they can edit themselves, live in two to three weeks.
Niches: cafés and bakeries; independent shops; trades (plumbers, electricians, joiners); B&Bs and small hotels; cleaners; florists; local gyms
